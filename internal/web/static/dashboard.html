    3

    Break a server

    Press a button on any card, then watch steps 2 and 4.

    Buttons
    • 🐢 Slow down every request takes +300ms, but health checks still pass
    • ⚠ 30% errors 3 in 10 requests fail
    • 💥 Crash 20s the server dies and restarts after 20 seconds
    • 🩺 Fail health its health check reports sick
    • 🔧 Repair undoes everything
    Server states
    • Healthy serving normally
    • Warming up back after a problem, gets traffic slowly
    • Ejected kept failing, taken out for a few seconds
    • Down not answering health checks, gets no traffic

    Who gets protected first

    With more traffic than the servers can handle, PulseLB turns away browsing first, then search. Bookings get through until the very end.
